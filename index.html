About

Hey there, I'm Brandon. I'm a musician and vocalist based in Northeast Florida. I play drums, guitar, and mandolin — drawing from a deep love of classic rock from the 60s and 70s, 90s and 2000s alt-rock, jam bands, bluegrass, and jazz. Whatever the stage, the goal's always the same: good music and a great time.
